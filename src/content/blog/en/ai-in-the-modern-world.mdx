# Artificial intelligence as a crutch in today's world

There is a very strong dependence on artificial intelligence today. It simplifies processes, expands possibilities, and makes tools for narration, development, writing, research, and many other areas more accessible.

## When AI becomes a shortcut

The problem is not simply using AI. The problem starts when it stops being a tool and becomes a shortcut used without understanding. If someone does not understand the process, they also do not fully understand what they are delivering.

## The risk of outsourcing thought

When we turn AI into an automatic extension of the brain, we stop exercising an essential part of cognition: building, interpreting, and developing something of our own. AI can support thinking, but it should not replace it.

## The bar also rises

AI makes many things easier, but it also raises the minimum expected standard. People with a solid foundation can use the tool better; people who only copy answers remain vulnerable when they need to evaluate, fix, or justify the result.

## A new literacy

Knowing how to interpret what AI produces has become a basic skill. We need to understand limits, risks, context, and responsibility, especially when artificial content starts to look indistinguishable from reality.

## Conclusion

In my view, AI is an important tool, but it does not replace training, worldview, or independent thinking. It should expand abilities, not become a crutch.
