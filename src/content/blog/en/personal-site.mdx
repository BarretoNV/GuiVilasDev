# My website

My website is a mix of many things: a resume, a career summary, an API playground, a personal blog, an astrophotography gallery, and a place for projects.

## Context

When I started writing this text, it had been almost a month since my thesis was approved, basically closing my Computer Engineering degree. That new free time opened room for hobbies, personal projects, and for waking up development skills that had been a little dormant.

## My place on the internet

Everyone has some kind of online space today, usually on social networks. But those platforms limit how much we can make things feel like our own. This website lets me build a small corner of the web on my own terms.

## Is it worth the effort?

It probably will not become a famous website with crowds reading my opinions. But the value is in having my own mark on the internet, learning new tools, and creating something that may feel nostalgic a few years from now.

## Conclusion

This website is a fun and productive hobby. I hope this space becomes something I can be proud of, and since it is an almost infinite playground, who knows what it might become.
