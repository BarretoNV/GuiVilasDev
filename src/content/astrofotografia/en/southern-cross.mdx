# The Southern Cross constellation

It is the smallest of the 88 modern constellations, but one of the brightest and most recognizable in the southern hemisphere.

## About the capture

This time I was more aware of how to photograph the sky and managed to use a more optimized setup. I had to hurry because some clouds were passing through, but it worked out in the end.

## About the target

This constellation appears in many places, including the Brazilian flag, and was widely used as a navigation guide during the Age of Exploration.
