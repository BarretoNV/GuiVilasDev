# Canis Major constellation

Canis Major is one of the brightest constellations in the night sky. Its best-known star is Sirius, the brightest star visible from Earth at night.

## About the capture

This was my first photo of this kind. The settings were not ideal and processing took some work, but the final result was satisfying.

## About the target

At that moment, the constellation was about 25º west in azimuth.
