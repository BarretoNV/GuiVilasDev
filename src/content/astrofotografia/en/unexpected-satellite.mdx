# ATLAS CENTAUR 2 satellite

While I was photographing the Southern Cross, one of the images captured a strange streak. Using software to analyze the sky with the date and time of the photo, I managed to identify that unexpected object.

## Curiosity

There are thousands of artificial satellites orbiting Earth, plus many fragments of space debris. Sometimes one of those objects decides to show up right in the middle of a photo.
